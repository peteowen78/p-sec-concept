Four places the money hides.

Psec can help you deliver revenue savings quickly, effectively and painlessly, allowing you to reduce your fixed cost base.

  • The estate valuation

    Modern Equivalent Assets. We optimise valuations using alternative sites, so you can reduce the balance sheet valuation, PDC payable and depreciation, and business rates.

    Contingent fee available

  • Creditor payments

    Our software database identifies duplicate payments, and enables root cause analysis to improve internal controls for payment systems.

    Contingent fee available

  • Salaries and wages

    Identifying incorrect salary and wages payments, as part of the same IT-enabled service.

    Daily rate or contingency

  • Capital VAT

    We review capital schemes from a VAT perspective: PFI contracts, leases, purchases and disposals of land and buildings, to maximise your capital VAT recovery.

    Daily rate or contingency
